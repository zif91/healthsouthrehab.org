Wellness & Health Resources

Last updated: December 2025

Navigating the vast landscape of health information requires access to reliable, evidence-based resources. Below, we've compiled professional organizations, research databases, quality health websites, and supplement safety resources to support your journey toward optimal health and wellness. These resources represent some of the most credible sources for medical and health information available online.

Professional Healthcare Organizations

Physical Therapy & Rehabilitation

American Physical Therapy Association (APTA)

Website: www.apta.org

The premier professional organization for physical therapists in the United States. APTA provides evidence-based resources, continuing education, professional standards, and public education about physical therapy. Their website includes a "Find a PT" tool to locate licensed physical therapists in your area and comprehensive information about PT specialties.

International Society of Physical Activity and Health (ISPAH)

Website: www.ispah.org

ISPAH promotes physical activity and exercise as essential components of health. Their resources include guidelines for physical activity recommendations across the lifespan and evidence about exercise's preventive health effects.

Pain Management & Mental Health

American Chronic Pain Association (ACPA)

Website: www.theacpa.org

ACPA provides comprehensive resources for chronic pain management, including peer support groups, education programs, and evidence-based information. Their website features resources on multimodal pain management, living with chronic pain, and advocacy.

American Psychological Association (APA)

Website: www.apa.org

APA's public resources section provides information on mental health conditions, evidence-based treatments including cognitive-behavioral therapy, stress management, and finding qualified mental health professionals. Their "Psychologist Locator" tool helps find licensed psychologists.

National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI)

Website: www.nami.org

NAMI provides comprehensive information on mental illness, recovery, treatment options, and support resources. Their peer-run support groups and educational programs offer valuable community connections and mental health education.

Holistic & Integrative Medicine

American Association of Naturopathic Physicians (AANP)

Website: www.naturopathic.org

AANP represents licensed naturopathic doctors and provides information about naturopathic medicine, botanical remedies, and nutritional approaches to health. Their website includes a practitioner directory and educational resources about natural medicine approaches.

American Holistic Health Association (AHHA)

Website: www.ahha.org

AHHA advocates for whole-person approaches to health combining conventional and complementary medicine. Their resources explore integrative health approaches, including mind-body medicine, stress management, and preventive health strategies.

Medical Research Databases & Evidence Sources

Research Databases

PubMed & MEDLINE

Website: pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov

PubMed provides free access to MEDLINE, the National Library of Medicine's database of biomedical literature. Search millions of peer-reviewed articles across all medical specialties. While technical, PubMed remains the gold standard for accessing primary research.

Cochrane Library

Website: www.cochranelibrary.com

Cochrane provides high-quality systematic reviews and meta-analyses of medical interventions. These reviews synthesize all available research on specific treatments, providing evidence-based conclusions about effectiveness. An excellent resource for understanding what research actually shows about health interventions.

Google Scholar

Website: scholar.google.com

Google Scholar indexes scholarly literature across many disciplines, including medicine and health sciences. Search for research articles, theses, and citations. Many articles are freely available, and the interface is user-friendly for non-specialists.

Reputable Health Information Websites

Government & Academic Medical Centers

National Institutes of Health (NIH)

Website: www.nih.gov

NIH provides evidence-based health information through multiple resources including MedlinePlus (consumer health information), research funding information, and health topic overviews developed by medical experts.

Mayo Clinic

Website: www.mayoclinic.org

Mayo Clinic's website provides comprehensive, evidence-based information on diseases, conditions, treatments, and healthy living. Content is developed by medical professionals and regularly updated based on current research.

Cleveland Clinic

Website: health.clevelandclinic.org

Cleveland Clinic's health library provides detailed information on medical conditions, treatments, procedures, and wellness topics, developed by healthcare professionals.

Johns Hopkins Medicine

Website: www.hopkinsmedicine.org

Johns Hopkins provides evidence-based health information and participates in cutting-edge medical research. Their website includes disease and treatment information from leading medical experts.

Government Health Agencies

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

Website: www.cdc.gov

CDC provides authoritative information on disease prevention, public health, immunizations, and health conditions. Their data and guidelines represent current evidence-based recommendations.

FDA - U.S. Food and Drug Administration

Website: www.fda.gov

FDA provides information on medication safety, approved drugs, supplements, and food safety. Their website includes drug information, warning updates, and consumer guidance.

National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH)

Website: www.nccih.nih.gov

NCCIH, part of NIH, provides evidence-based information on complementary and integrative health approaches, including botanical medicine, acupuncture, and mind-body therapies.

Supplement & Botanical Safety Resources

Supplement Quality & Safety

Natural Medicines Database

Website: naturalmedicines.therapeuticresearch.com

Natural Medicines provides evidence-based information on herbal medicines, supplements, and botanical products. They rate effectiveness and safety, identify drug interactions, and provide dosing information. This is a professional-level resource with excellent scientific rigor.

ConsumerLab.com

Website: www.consumerlab.com

ConsumerLab independently tests supplements and botanical products for quality, purity, and accuracy of label claims. Their test results identify products that pass or fail quality standards, helping consumers choose reliable supplements.

U.S. Pharmacopeia (USP)

Website: www.usp.org

USP sets standards for pharmaceutical quality and provides verification of supplements meeting high quality standards. Look for "USP Verified" mark on supplement bottles to ensure quality and purity.

NSF International

Website: www.nsf.org

NSF certifies supplements for quality, purity, and label accuracy. NSF-certified products undergo rigorous testing and are regularly monitored for quality assurance.

Specific Health Topics

Website: www.mindful.org

Provides information on mindfulness practice, meditation, and stress management. Includes research summaries, guided practices, and expert articles on mind-body wellness.

Nutrition & Dietary Health

Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics

Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health - Nutrition Source

Provides nutrition information based on Harvard research. Includes information on various foods, nutrients, diet patterns, and disease prevention through nutrition.

Resources for Finding Quality Healthcare Providers

Website: www.healthgrades.com

Search for doctors, providers, and hospitals in your area. Includes provider credentials, specialties, insurance information, and patient reviews.

Zocdoc

Website: www.zocdoc.com

Find and book appointments with healthcare providers. Includes provider information, patient reviews, insurance verification, and appointment availability.

Board Certification Check

Website: www.abms.org

Verify if your physician is board-certified through ABMS (American Board of Medical Specialties). Board certification indicates completion of rigorous specialty training.

How to Evaluate Health Information

Critical Questions for Assessing Health Information

  • Who wrote it? Is the author qualified and credible? Are they affiliated with a reputable organization?
  • What's the purpose? Is the goal to educate or to sell a product? Is there a financial conflict of interest?
  • Is it evidence-based? Does it cite scientific research? Are claims supported by evidence?
  • Is it current? When was it written? Has it been recently updated?
  • What's the balance? Does it present multiple perspectives? Does it acknowledge limitations and uncertainties?
  • Is it personalized? Remember that general information doesn't apply to everyone. Consult with your healthcare provider about what applies to your specific situation.

Additional Resources

The resources listed above represent excellent starting points for reliable, evidence-based health information. Remember that while online resources provide valuable education, they cannot replace personalized medical evaluation and care from qualified healthcare professionals. Use these resources to become informed about your health, but always consult with your healthcare providers for medical decisions specific to your situation.
