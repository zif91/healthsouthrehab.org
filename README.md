# HealthSouth Rehabilitation & Wellness Center

> An evidence-based informational portal about rehabilitation, recovery, and wellness approaches.

## 🎯 Concept

HealthSouth Rehabilitation & Wellness Center is a comprehensive information portal focused on:

- **Rehabilitation Methods**: Evidence-based physical therapy and recovery techniques
- **Alternative Medicine**: Botanical therapies and complementary wellness approaches
- **Wellness Education**: Research-backed health information and wellness trends
- **Condition Management**: Chronic pain, stress management, and holistic health
