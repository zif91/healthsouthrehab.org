## Evaluating Health Information Quality

When researching health information online, use these criteria to assess quality:

### ✓ Credibility Checklist

- **Author Credentials**: Information written or reviewed by qualified healthcare professionals
- **Citations Provided**: Claims backed by references to peer-reviewed research
- **Date Current**: Content recently published or updated
- **Unbiased**: Clear disclosure of funding sources and conflicts of interest
- **Purpose Clear**: Educational rather than primarily commercial
- **Privacy Protected**: Secure handling of personal health information
- **Contact Information**: Legitimate organization with verifiable contact details

### 🚩 Red Flags to Watch For

- Promises of miraculous cures or guaranteed results
- Claims that one treatment works for all diseases
- Pressure to make immediate decisions
- Discouragement from seeking conventional medical care
- Lack of scientific references or "secret formulas"
- Conspiracy theories about medical establishment
- Anonymous authors or organizations
- Testimonials replacing scientific evidence
